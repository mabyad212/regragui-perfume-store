# Regragui Perfume — Full catalogue package

- Replaces the 31 temporary demo entries with the full fragrance catalogue transcribed from the uploaded catalogue pages.
- No duplicate product entries are intentionally included.
- Product visuals are individual, composition-led Regragui bottle scenes. Men's bottles are black; women's bottles are white. Each bottle label says REGRAGUI PERFUME only; the inspiration brand/name appears outside the bottle in the product card.
- Prices are randomly generated indicative MAD prices and must be reviewed before publishing.
- Inspiration references are catalogue descriptors, not claims of affiliation.

Open `index.html` locally or deploy the `regragui-perfume-store` folder to Vercel.
